Contact: finn@summitflow.co.uk  |  07949 845 919

2. What data we collect

When you submit the application form on our website, we collect:

  • Full name and business name

We also collect standard technical data automatically when you visit our site (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via server logs and any analytics tools we use.

3. Why we collect it (lawful basis)

We process your personal data on the following bases:

  • Legitimate interests — to respond to your enquiry, assess whether we can help your business, and contact you about SummitFlow services you have expressed interest in.
  • Contract — where you become a client, to fulfil our service obligations.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain records under applicable law.

4. How we use your data

  • To respond to your application or enquiry
  • To assess your suitability for our service
  • To contact you about our services (by phone, email, or message)
  • To onboard and manage you as a client
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will not sell your data to third parties. We will not use it for unrelated marketing without your explicit consent.

5. Who we share data with

We may share your data with:

  • Formspree — our form submission processor (processes data on our behalf)
  • Go High Level — our CRM platform
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — where you become a client and we run campaigns on your behalf
  • AppointWise — our AI appointment-setting platform

All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements and handle data in accordance with UK GDPR.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiry / application data: up to 12 months if no contract is formed
  • Client data: for the duration of the contract and up to 6 years after for legal and accounting purposes

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Data portability (where applicable)

To exercise any of these rights, email finn@summitflow.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.
