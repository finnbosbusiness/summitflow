01 — Ideal Client Profile

MCS-certified heat pump installers ready to scale

Targeting established installation businesses with the capability to convert surveys but without the systems to generate a consistent pipeline.

🎯

Target Niche

MCS-certified heat pump installers across the UK. Companies with trained engineers doing air source and ground source heat pump installations. Eligible to complete Boiler Upgrade Scheme installations (£7,500 government grant).

📊

Company Profile

Owner-operated businesses with 2–15 engineers. Revenue of £200k–£2M annually. Owner is accessible as the decision-maker and actively involved in business development. Likely trades under their own brand, not a franchise.

Core Pain Points

  • Inconsistent survey pipeline — feast or famine, especially in warmer months when installs should peak
  • Reliance on manufacturer leads — Vaillant, Worcester Bosch, Mitsubishi leads are inconsistent and shared with competitors
  • Word-of-mouth dependency — referrals dry up. No predictable lead generation system in place
  • Engineers sitting idle — expensive trained workforce with no enough surveys to fill the week
  • Missing BUS grant demand — homeowners searching for MCS installers to get the £7,500 grant can't find them easily
  • No time for marketing — owner is on-site or managing jobs, not generating new business
  • Survey no-shows — wasting engineer time on unqualified or uncommitted homeowners

Job Value

£8,000–£18,000 per installation (with BUS grant reducing homeowner cost to £3,500–£10,500). A single install covers months of SummitFlow retainer.

Awareness Level

Level 2–3: Problem aware and solution aware. Know they need more surveys. Most have tried marketing or manufacturer leads. Few aware of AI appointment setting.

Easy to Reach

MCS Certificate database (public). Companies House. Google Maps. LinkedIn. Decision-maker typically answers the office phone directly.

💡

Why MCS is the perfect niche: The MCS database is public and gives you a verified, qualified list of every eligible installer in the UK. You're not guessing who to call — you know exactly who can deliver the service and who qualifies to do BUS grant work.

07 — Key Principles

The mindset that makes it work

On Fear

"It is completely normal to be riddled with fear when it comes to cold calling. We are dedicating time in our day to be told no again and again. Everything you want to achieve is on the other side of that fear barrier." — Jordan Platten

On Volume

100 calls/day is not optional. At 4% meeting rate you need 25 conversations. 25 conversations require 80–100 dials. The maths only works if you show up every day. Volume solves everything.

On Imposter Syndrome

"You are not going to break through imposter syndrome until you sign up your first couple of clients, deliver great results, and they tell you they're happy. Imposter syndrome indicates growth, not evidence to stop." — Jordan Platten

On the Runway

Runway ends August 19. That's roughly 10 weeks. One client = the runway extended. Three clients = sustainable. The only thing that closes clients is calls made. Everything else is noise.
