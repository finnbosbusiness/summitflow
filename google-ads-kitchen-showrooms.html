Do you guarantee a number of appointments?

Our current offer is 20 qualified design appointments within the first 90 days. If we fall short, we continue working without our management fee until the agreed target is reached, subject to the terms agreed before launch. Advertising spend remains payable.

How much should a kitchen showroom spend on Google Ads?

There is no single suitable budget for every showroom. It depends on the towns you serve, competitive search demand, your projects and your ability to handle enquiries. We discuss a budget based on your situation before campaigns launch.

Do you only focus on clicks and website traffic?

No. Traffic is only useful if it leads to relevant conversations. We focus on enquiry quality and booked design appointments, and use those outcomes to inform campaign decisions where tracking allows.

Who controls the advertising account?

The intention is for campaigns to run through an account your business controls, with access to campaign data. We’ll confirm the account setup before any work begins.

Does the guarantee include ad spend?

No. Advertising spend is separate and remains payable to the advertising platform. The guarantee relates to our management fee and the agreed appointment target.

What counts as a qualified design appointment?

We agree the qualification criteria with your showroom before launch. They should reflect the homeowner’s project, service area and other practical requirements, plus a genuine booking for a design appointment.

Do appointments guarantee sales?

No. The commitment is to qualified appointments, not sales. The final sale depends on the customer, your offer, pricing, design process and sales team.
