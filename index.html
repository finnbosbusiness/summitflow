The work is coming. The question is who fits it.

600k a year

The target. Nobody is close to it.

That's the government's own number for 2028. Actual output has been running at a fraction of it since the target was set. The gap does not close by itself, and it does not close without more fitted jobs per firm.

Roughly a threefold increase in two years, rising toward 60,000 by 2035. The trade isn't short of work coming. It's short of firms big enough to take it.

Questions

Before you get on the call.

What counts as a qualified survey?

Homeowner, property in your radius, actively looking to switch, aware of rough costs, and they picked the slot themselves. Anything short of that doesn't count toward the number and you aren't billed for it.

What does it cost?

A monthly retainer plus your ad budget, which goes straight to the platforms - we never mark it up. Both get quoted on the area call, once we've looked at your postcodes.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Everything is built in your business name on your accounts. If you leave, the account, the data and the audiences stay with you.

What if my area is taken?

We'll tell you on the call and put you on the list for it. We won't take your money to run in an area we've already sold.
