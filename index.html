Questions

Good questions. Straight answers.

How we work, what it costs and what you can expect — explained clearly, with no guesswork.

Work with us
What counts as a qualified appointment?

A suitable homeowner who is actively considering a new kitchen, fits the criteria agreed with your showroom and has booked a design appointment. We’ll define the qualification criteria with you before launch.

What does it cost?

You pay a management fee plus your advertising budget. Your ad spend goes directly to the platform. We’ll discuss the recommended budget and management fee based on your showroom and objectives.

Who owns the ad account?

Your campaigns are managed through an advertising account you control. You retain access to your campaigns and their data.

Do you work with competing showrooms?

We aim to limit the number of showrooms we work with in a given area. Any exclusivity, including the territory it covers, must be agreed explicitly before work begins.

How is this different from buying leads?

We’re focused on more than generating enquiries. We follow up with potential customers and work to get suitable design appointments booked into your diary, rather than leaving you with a list of people to chase.

How soon will appointments start coming through?

The first stage involves setting up the campaigns, tracking and enquiry-handling process. Timing depends on your market, advertising budget and campaign performance, so we’ll set expectations based on your specific situation.

Do you guarantee kitchen sales?

No. The offer is based on an agreed number of qualified appointments, not sales. Whether an appointment becomes a sale depends on the homeowner’s requirements, your pricing, your design process and your sales team.

What is your appointment guarantee?

The current offer is 15 qualified design appointments within the first 90 days. If we don’t deliver, we continue managing the campaigns without our management fee until the agreed target is reached, subject to the agreed terms. Advertising spend is separate.
