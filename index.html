What counts as a qualified survey?

Homeowner, in your radius, actively looking, knows roughly what it costs, and picked the slot themselves. Anything less isn't billed.

What does it cost?

A monthly retainer plus your ad budget. The budget goes straight to the platforms, never marked up. Both quoted on the call.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Built in your business name, on your accounts. If you leave, all of it stays with you.

What if my area is taken?

We tell you straight away and put you on the list. We won't take your money to run somewhere we've already sold.

How is this different from buying leads?

Lead sellers sell the same enquiry to four installers and let you race each other to the phone. Nothing here is shared, resold or recycled. One homeowner, one installer, one slot already in your diary.

How soon do the first surveys land?

Week one is setup: ad accounts, tracking, creative and your booking calendar. Bookings usually start in the second or third week, once the platforms have enough data to find your buyers.

Do I have to be MCS certified?

Yes, or be part-way through it. Grant funding depends on it and homeowners ask on the first call, so we don't take installers who can't answer that.
